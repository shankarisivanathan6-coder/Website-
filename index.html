Process

How it works

  1. 1

    Brief

    You send the product, who it's for, and what you need it to do. I come back with a couple of concepts before anything gets filmed.

  2. 2

    Shoot

    I film it myself, at home or on location. You'll see bits of it along the way, so nothing at the end comes as a surprise.

  3. 3

    Edit

    Cut, captioned, graded and mixed. You get it in the sizes and lengths your channels actually use.

  4. 4

    Revise

    Every package includes a round of revisions. Extra hooks or a re-cut are a flat add-on, so you know the cost before you ask.

About

Hi, I'm Shankari.

I'm a creator based between San Francisco and Toronto. I came to video through photography, and I still work the same way: most of what I make starts with noticing one small thing and building the video around it.

I shoot and edit everything myself. Phone footage when it should look like phone footage, and a Fujifilm X100VI when a shot is worth it. Doing all of it myself is slower, but it's the reason the finished video still sounds like a person.

If there's a real reason your product exists, that's the kind of thing I like working on. Send me the details and I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right fit.

FAQ

Good things to know

What's the turnaround?

Usually 7 to 10 days from when the product arrives, depending on the package. I can turn it around in 48 hours as a rush add-on.

Do you need the product sent to you?

For physical products, yes. I'd rather use something properly before filming it, and you can usually tell on camera when someone hasn't. For apps, account access is fine.

Who owns the footage?

You own the delivered videos for organic posting. Paid ads and whitelisting are covered for 30 days on Signature, and longer usage is a flat add-on.

Will you appear on camera?

Yes, when it helps. Some products do better with hands-only or plain B-roll, and I'll say so if I think that's the case with yours.

Do you post it to your own channels?

Everything is delivered for you to post. If you want it going out on my accounts too, that's a separate add-on and I'd quote it per campaign.
